AI tools, explained

Short overviews of widely used AI tools. Features and pricing change often, so always check each official website for current details. Tharun Tech Hub is not affiliated with or endorsed by any company listed here.

Assistant

ChatGPT (OpenAI)

A general-purpose AI chat assistant for writing, brainstorming, coding help and analysis.

  • Best for: drafting, summarising, learning topics
  • Use case: turn rough notes into a clear email
Assistant

Claude (Anthropic)

An AI assistant known for working with long documents and careful writing.

  • Best for: reading reports, editing, coding help
  • Use case: summarise a long PDF and list action items
Assistant

Google Gemini

Google's AI assistant, connected to Google's ecosystem of products.

  • Best for: people already using Google apps
  • Use case: brainstorm ideas, then organise them
Research

Perplexity

An AI-powered search tool that answers questions with source links.

  • Best for: quick research with citations
  • Use case: find sources for a student assignment
Design

Canva (Magic tools)

Design platform with built-in AI features for graphics and presentations.

  • Best for: creators and small businesses
  • Use case: social posts and simple slide decks
Notes

Notion AI

AI features inside the Notion workspace for notes and documents.

  • Best for: organised notes and team docs
  • Use case: clean up meeting notes
Tip: AI can make mistakes. Verify important facts, and never paste private or confidential information into any AI tool.
