What is Dub?

Link management

Dub (dub.co)

Dub is a link management platform. It lets you turn long URLs into short, branded links and see how those links perform.

  • Shorten links: create short, shareable URLs
  • Understand performance: view clicks and where traffic comes from
  • Organise: keep many links in one dashboard
  • Use case: share one link on WhatsApp or social media and see which channel gets clicks
